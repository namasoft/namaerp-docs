# Business Scenarios

The module pages explain one screen at a time; these pages start from the kind of business instead — a distributor selling on orders, a retail chain with registers, a developer selling units off-plan. Each one says who the scenario fits, which licences it needs, how the documents flow from the first one to the cash, the order to set things up in, and the decisions to settle before go-live, then points you to the module pages for the detail.

Most companies combine two scenarios, such as a distributor that also imports its goods, so read both pages.
